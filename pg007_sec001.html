Introduction to Sport Studies

Introduction

Sport Studies play an important role in student’s physical, mental and social development. In Sport Studies, students are provided with the opportunities to participate in a variety of physical activities including sports and games for leisure and competition purposes. In this chapter, you will learn the concept and elements of Sport Studies, categories and functions of sports, importance of studying Sport Studies and its relationship with other disciplines. The competencies developed will enable you to appreciate the values of sports and develop interest of participating in various sports activities.

Concept of Sport Studies

In this book, the term Sport Studies is used synonymously with Physical Education (PE). Sport Studies is the study of sports and the associated human movements. It refers to an academic discipline that provides a platform for cognitive and motor skills, character and behavioural development for individual well-being and social changes through physical activities and sports. Sport Studies subject aims to develop students’ physical competence, sport skills, knowledge of movement, safety skills, and its applications in performing a wide range of activities associated with sports and healthy lifestyle. Thus, the subject remains a vital part of the total process of education that promotes active lifestyles and enables individual to participate in lifelong sports, exercise and recreational activities during leisure time.
