Burpees

Burpees target your shoulders, thighs, and core muscles, burning many calories in the process. You can use a mat if desired. The following is how you can perform it:

(i) Start by holding the body up horizontally in the plank position with the toes and palms on the ground;

(ii) Tuck the knees, bring them up to the chest and plant the feet on either side of the hands;

(iii) Drive the weight through the heels to jump into an upright stance to complete a single repetition; and
