Safety practices in sports

Safety practices in sports are rules that cautions an athlete, which if well considered tends to prevent injuries and related risks in exercises and sporting activities. Some of these require athletes to:

  • (a) Wear appropriate sport attires;
  • (b) Assess the safety of playing ground and facilities before starting activity;
  • (c) Perform proper warm-up and cool down;
  • (d) Use safe and correct equipment;
  • (e) Use equipment with much caution;
  • (f) Do not continue practising when you are tired or injured;
  • (g) Make sure there is another person while exercising;
  • (h) Adopt the right posture and technique;
  • (i) Hydrate your body by drinking enough water;
  • (j) Obey the governing rules and regulations of the particular sport;
  • (k) Seek for medical assistance if you are injured;
  • (l) Get enough time for recovery after exercises or sport activity; and
  • (m) Use appropriate protective equipment.
