Law no.15: The Throw-in

When the ball crosses the sideline and an opposing player was the last to touch it, the throwing is given to the player of another team. The ball is thrown from the spot where it crossed the line. The thrower must face the playing field, have both feet planted, toss the ball over his head with two hands from behind, and use two hands. If a throw-in results in the ball entering the goal without being touched by another player, a goal kick is given.

Law no.16: The Goal-kick

When the ball crosses the goal line of the other team’s goal and no goals have been scored, the defending team is given a goal-kick. The goal-kick may be taken by any player, who then places the ball wherever in the goal area. After a kick, once the ball has not been touched by another player from either team, the taker is not permitted to touch it again.

Law no.17: Corner-kick

When the opposing side is the last to touch the ball, and it crosses the goal line without a goal being scored, the attacking team is given a corner. Play is resumed by the attacking team kicking the ball from the corner arc closest to the goal line.
