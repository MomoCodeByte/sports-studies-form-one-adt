Fitness in sports

Introduction

Fitness is an important element of body healthy and performance in various activities. In the light of the contemporary emphasis on promoting lifetime wellness, physical fitness should become central in a mindset of every human being. Having fitness skills helps people to improve their health, as well as enhancing performance. In this chapter, you will learn the meaning, importance, attributes and components of mental and physical fitness. The competencies developed will enable you to improve your health and perform well in sports and other activities involved.

Concept of fitness

Fitness is the person’s entire physical and mental well-being. It is achieved by the combination of regular physical activity, a healthy diet, appropriate rest, and other healthy lifestyle choices. It is a state of physical and mental well-being in which an individual’s body can accomplish duties and activities effectively and efficiently. Fitness in sports can be categorised into physical and mental fitness. For exercises to be a tool for body fitness, it must be performed well to avoid harming body parts and systems. Therefore, a proper exercise must start by a warm up and end by a cool down.
