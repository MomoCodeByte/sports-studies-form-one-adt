Skills and techniques in playing football

Football requires a combination of various skills to have a good performance in the game. Movement skills with the ball and without the ball are important in football. Techniques without the ball include running, jumping, change of direction, landing, downfall and positioning. Techniques performed with the ball are ball control, passing, dribbling, shooting, heading, tackling and goalkeeping. The following are the descriptions of various techniques with the ball needed in playing football.

Ball controlling

Ball control is the ability to grab the ball from the ground or the air and maintain possession of it whether for passing, dribbling, or shooting. High levels of attention and a good body posture are needed for this ability. Usually, the foot, thigh, chest, and head are used to control balls coming in the air. The ball’s trajectory, your location, and the position of the opponent all determine which part of the body should be used to control it.
