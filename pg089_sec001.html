Action of the leading leg

The leading knee is lifted straight up until the thigh is just below. This gives the stability when landing and controlling the whole body, hence relaxing muscles before taking the next stride. A perfect execution helps to run for a long time without undue fatigue.

Action of rear leg

The back leg must be in an extended position before it leaves the ground. This gives the body balance and stability when running. The leg muscles contract and generate energy for lifting the body weight and supporting a runner to keep on running.

Finishing 800 m race

The 800 m race always ends with a finishing sprint. The runner increases his speed by sprinting towards the finishing line with vigorous stride and increasing the stride length in line with speed.
