Relays

Relay race is a track event in which athletes run in teams of four runners each. Each runner of a team runs a section or a leg of race in a track while exchanging baton in a specified zone. The team whose fourth runner finishes first, wins a race. Relay involves four phases, namely starting, running, baton exchange and finishing. Within each phase, there are various skills to be performed by participants. The most common relay races are the 4x100 m and 4x 400 m. Relays require equipment such as baton, pistol or clappers, flags and stopwatch.

The fundamental rules in relay

(a) Baton should be exchanged in the exchanging zones and not otherwise; (b) Do not pass the baton by throwing to the outgoing runner; and

(c) Run within the assigned lane throughout the race.
