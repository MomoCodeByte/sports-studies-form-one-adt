Health related physical fitness

The health related physical fitness comprises of five key components which must be addressed to promote total body fitness, namely, body composition, flexibility, muscular endurance, muscular strength and cardiovascular endurance.

Body composition

Body composition is the proportion of lean body tissue (such as muscles, bones, water and connective tissues) and fat in the body. It refers to the percentage of each tissue or substance present in the body and the ratios they form with one another. Body composition can be measured using methods such as Body Mass Index (BMI), skinfold and bioelectrical impedance. The proper way to maintain body composition is to exercise regularly and eating well.

Exercises for improving body composition

Although people can share the same type of physical activity, the intensity and duration of exercise differ from one individual to another depending on the ability and health needs. Here are some body composition exercises that can help maintain, decrease and increase your percentage of adipose tissue and lean body mass to achieve a better balance between the two. Most of these exercises require either no equipment at all or simple equipment, such as free weights, skipping ropes, mats and cones.

Sit-ups and Crunches

Sit-ups and crunches are exercises that burn calories and work the abdominal muscles, which help to increase muscle size. Sit-ups and crunches both start the same way. The following is how you can perform the exercise:

(i) Lie on the back with the feet flat on the ground, the knees elevated, and the hands either behind the head or at the opposite shoulders;

(ii) To do crunches, engage the muscles of the core to elevate the head and shoulders off the ground while exhaling then inhale while releasing them back to the ground; and

(iii) To do sit-ups, engage multiple groups of muscles to curl the upper body all the way up to the knees before releasing during exhalation.
