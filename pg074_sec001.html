Activity 5.7

Practise proper finishing in sprints.

Question:

What are the criteria used to judge that the runner crossed the finishing line?

Fundamental rules in sprints

(a)

You should have appropriate jersey and shoes. Failure to observe these rule will make you disqualified from the competition;

(b)

Make sure that you have registered and provided with the competition number and lane number;

(c)

Do not take-off the block before the final command (pistol or clapper); and

(d)

Run on the assigned lane throughout the race.
