Concept of safety

In any physical exercise and sports, safety should be considered important in all aspects at all ages and levels of learning. The beauty of sports and exercises can be affected by accidents and injuries. It is therefore, important that everyone acquires safety skills to prevent unnecessary injuries in physical activities and sports.

Why safety skills?

The safety skills help to:

(a)Reduce the risk of injuries in physical activities;
(b)Develop ability to manage injuries;
