Introduction

Track events are running races that take place in a 400 m oval running track. The events include short distance (sprints, relays and hurdles), middle and long distance racing. These events can be performed for competition and recreation purposes. Track events help people to get opportunities for employment as well as promoting active life style and social skills. In this chapter, you will learn safety measures, skills, and rules applied in track events. The competencies developed will enable you to analyse, officiate and perform track events skillfully.
