Warming up and Cooling down

Before and after every physical activity, an individual is required to do some simple activities for safety which are warming up and cooling down respectively.

Warming up

Warm-up is crucial for the body before exercising or playing sport. It prepares the body and mind for forthcoming activity. The warming up aims to:

  • (a) Gradually raise the temperature of the body;
  • (b) Increase mental awareness; and
  • (c) Increase the range of motion of the body joints.

Every warming up consist of:

  • (a) General warm-up, that focuses to prepare the body systems via aerobic activities and mobility exercises;
  • (b) Specific warm-up, that focuses on activity-related movements to prepare the muscle for the main activity; and
  • (c) Stretching, this focuses on preparing the muscles and their associated tissues.

Carrying out a warm-up

A warm-up should not leave an individual tired. To carry out a proper warm-up one can do the following for at least 10 - 15 minutes:

  • (i) Walk by increasing your speed for at least 3 minutes;
  • (ii) Jog slowly for at least 3 minutes;
  • (iii) Add some movements while jogging such as arm circling, knee ups, leg-back and heels kicking your buttocks alternately for at least 3 minutes;
  • (iv) Jog slowly and then walk for at least 3 minutes; and
