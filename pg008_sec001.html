Sport Studies as a subject uses a number of terms. These terms include, movement, posture, performance, and healthy or active lifestyle.

Movement

Movement encompasses a broad range of skills that form the foundation for successful participation in games, sports and exercises. These skills can be categorised into locomotive, non-locomotive and manipulative. Locomotive skills include, walking, running, jumping and landing, hopping, leaping, sliding and dodging, while nonlocomotive skills include, bending and stretching, twisting and turning, pushing and pulling as well as swinging and swaying. On the other hand manipulative skills include, throwing, catching, striking, dribbling, kicking and volleying.

Posture

Posture is the bodily positioning of a person during rest, standing or moving. Good posture entails a correct positioning and proper alignment that provide the individual with a comfortable sensation. Therefore, you have to execute a proper posture in every physical activity you do for safety and precise performance.

Performance

Performance is the use of physical and mental effort to produce effective outcomes when participating in physical activity. Performance in sports refers to the ability to demonstrate proficiency and skills in a particular sport.

Healthy or active-lifestyle

A healthy or active-lifestyle refers to a way of living that promotes physical, mental, and emotional well-being of an individual through regular participation in physical activities, eating balanced diet, and positive lifestyle choices. It encompasses a variety of habits and behaviours that contribute to overall health and wellbeing. Healthy movements may include walking, sporting, dancing, yoga and running. For a healthy living, active life should be coupled with eating a well-balanced diet. The emphasis is on encouraging consuption of low-fat diet with plenty of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains.
