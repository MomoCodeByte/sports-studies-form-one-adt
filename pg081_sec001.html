Finishing 4x400 m relay

Similar to 4x100 m relay, the 4x400 m relay ends when the final runner in a team crosses the finishing line with any part of the body. Usually, the fastest runner in each group has a duty to finish the last portion of the 4x400 m relay with the baton on the hand.

Hurdles

Hurdles are running events in which an athlete jumps over obstacles at speed continuously to the finishing point. The major types of hurdle race are 110 m and 400 m for men, and 100 m for women. Hurdles involve three phases, namely starting, running and finishing. Within each phase, there are various skills to be performed by participants.
