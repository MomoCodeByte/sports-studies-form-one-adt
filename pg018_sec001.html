Chapter Two

Safety in sports

Introduction

When entering a sport ground, a court, or a track, keep in mind that safety is more than just a precaution. Safety forms the basis for unforgettable sporting experiences. In this chapter, you will learn the meaning of safety and its importance, sports injuries and their causes, as well as safety practices towards participants’ safety and successful performance. The competencies developed will enhance your ability to perform various sports safely and ensure a responsible athletic attitude.

The effect of failure to observe safety in sports activities

Concept of safety

In any physical exercise and sports, safety should be considered important in all aspects at all ages and levels of learning. The beauty of sports and exercises can be affected by accidents and injuries. It is therefore, important that everyone acquires safety skills to prevent unnecessary injuries in physical activities and sports.

Why safety skills?
