Sport Studies for Secondary Schools: Student's Book Form One

Glossary terms and their definitions for key words used in the book.
Hydration maintaining adequate fluid intake to support optimal physical performance.
Intensity refers to the level of effort or energy exerted during physical activity or exercise.
Motor skills abilities to perform physical activities using specific movements.
Offence an act of the team with possession of the ball, trying to score or attack opponent goal.
Pass sending the ball to a teammate.
Physical education a subject that focuses on physical fitness and sports.
Physical fitness the ability to perform physical activities effectively and efficiently.
Protective gear equipment used to protect the body during sports activities.
Relay a race in which team members take turns running specific distances.
Sedentary lifestyle a lifestyle with little or no physical activity.
Sportsmanship ethical and fair behaviour in sports and physical activities.
Sprint a short-distance race at top speed.
Strength the capacity of muscles to exert force; improved through resistance training.
Stretching refers to a set of exercises or movements that involve elongating and lengthening the muscles to increase flexibility and range of motion.
Tackle stopping the opposing player with the ball by forcing them to the ground.
Track event a running events conducted in 400 m oval running track.
Eating well a practice of getting a balanced diet in a light amount and time in relation to individual’s requirement.
Skinfold a test that estimates the amount of the body fat.
Technique a way of carrying out a particular skill.
Bioelectrical impedance a measurement of body composition based on the degree of resistance of flow of electric current passed through the body.
