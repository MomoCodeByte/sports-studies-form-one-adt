Cardiovascular endurance

Cardiovascular endurance is sometimes known as cardiorespiratory endurance. This refers to the ability of the heart and lungs to work together to provide the needed oxygen to the body during sustained workloads such as jogging, cycling and swimming. Improved cardiovascular endurance allows smooth flour of oxygenated blood. The Cooper test is commonly used to test cardiovascular endurance.

Exercises for cardiovascular endurance

Some exercises which can improve cardiovascular endurance are rope skipping, dancing, vigorous distance running, swimming and cycling.
