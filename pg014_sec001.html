Importance of studying Sport Studies

Sport Studies provide students with competencies and the attitude necessary to take part in a range of physical activities and become a central part of their life in school and out of school. The importance of Sport Studies are outlined below: (a) Help to keep an individual fit and healthy; (b) Help to develop self-confidence;

(c) Build a committed and responsible person;

(d) Help an individual to develop basic motor skills; (e) Help an individual to play various sports; (f) Help an individual to develop concentration and raise-up academic performance; (g) Brings enjoyment to a person; (h) Enhance creativity to an individual;

(i) Help to manage stress; and (j) Help an individual to be employed, self-employed or employ others.
