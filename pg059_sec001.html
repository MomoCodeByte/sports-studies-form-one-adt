Tackling

Tackling is a technique used to intercept the opponent’s ball. It is performed by trailing position, from the side of or behind the opponent. The aspects to consider in executing a tackle is timing, target and balance. Balance helps to recover and coordinate the body movements to avoid falling after performing a tackle.
