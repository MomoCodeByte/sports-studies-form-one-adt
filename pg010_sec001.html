Dance

Dance is a form of artistic expression achieved by physical activity that involves rhythmic movements of the body, often accompanied by music. It involves various dancing movements, techniques, and routines as a means of promoting physical fitness, coordination, and overall well-being of an individual.

Categories of sports

Sports encompass various types of competitive physical activities such as football, swimming, tennis and wrestling. Sports can be classified basing on different criteria including, the equipment used (racket sports, ball sports, stick sports, water sports, etc) and competition between players (individual sports, partner sports and team sports). In this section, you will learn the classification of sports based on competition between players only. When the classification is based on competition between players, there are three categories of sports: (a) Individual sports; (b) Partner sports; and

(c) Team sports.

Individual sports

These are the types of sports in which competitors do not rely on others to achieve their goals, rather, they do so at their own pace, as shown in Figure 1.1. For example, Athletics (except relay), boxing, cycling, darts, golf, rock climbing, wrestling, tennis and table tennis singles.
