Introduction

Football is a team sport played by using feet to kick a ball into the opponent’s goal to score. This game is played for competition and recreation purposes. Playing Football helps people to get opportunity for employment, improving their health as well as acquiring life skills. In this chapter, you will learn safety measures, skills, and rules applied in football. The competencies developed will enable you to analyse and play football skillfully.

Concept of football

Football is a ball game played by two opposing teams each with 11 players. The objective of the game is to hit a ball with feet into the opponent’s goal to score. The game is played for 90 minutes that is divided into two halves of 45 minutes by a 15 minutes’ break. The team that scores more goals wins the game.
