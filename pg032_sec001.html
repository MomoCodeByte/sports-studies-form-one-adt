Carrying out a cool down

To perform a proper cool down exercise, you can do the following for at least 10 minutes:

(i) Jog slowly for about 2 minutes;

(ii) Walk normal for about 2 minutes;

(iii) Get deep breathing in and out for about 1 minute; and

(iv) Stretch the whole body either up-down or down-up for about 5 minutes.

Mental fitness

Mental fitness enables a student to cope with pressure of academic life at school, adapt to changing environment at home and school, also to have positive view of life in general. As one participates in exercises and sports he or she acquires knowledge and skills for social, health, emotional and cognitive development.

Physical fitness

The total body wellness depends on the level of physical fitness. Physical fitness is the capacity of body organs and related systems (heart, blood vessels, lung and muscles) to function closer to the maximum efficiency. Once an individual participates in exercises to become physically fit, the mind also becomes fit.
