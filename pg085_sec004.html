The 110 m hurdles

The 110 m hurdles is a specialised event for men. In this event, an athlete must run a distance of 110 m on a track while jumping the obstacles (hurdles) placed on the lane. The athlete must clear 10 hurdles, each with a height of 106.7 cm, placed 13.72 m from the starting line and spaced 9.14 m between each hurdle, and 14.0 m from the last hurdle to the finish hurdle. All fundamental skills are similar to 100 m hurdling.

The 400 m hurdles

The 400 m hurdles is a specialised event for men. In this event, an athlete must sprint 400 m on a track while avoiding the obstacles (hurdles) placed on the lane. The athlete clears 10 hurdles, each with a height of 91.4 cm placed 45 m from the starting line to the first hurdle and spaced 35 m between each hurdle, and 40 m between the last hurdle in the finishing line. All fundamental skills in the 400 m hurdle are similar to 100 m hurdling.
