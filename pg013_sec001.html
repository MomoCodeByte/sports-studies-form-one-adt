1. (a)

    Entertainment: Sports are perceived as a source of amusement where individuals derive pleasure and alleviate stress by either engaging in or observing sporting events;

  2. (b)

    Through sports people can learn various aspects which are useful in daily life such as:

    1. (i)

      Focus: Engaging in sports improves your ability to concentrate on the present moment, leaving the past behind as insignificant and the future as uncertain;

    2. (ii)

      Valuing the process: Engaging in sports instills the valuable lesson of prioritising the process over the outcome. When the process is handled with diligence, achievement and success naturally follow;

    3. (iii)

      Tolerance: Sports instill in you the value of tolerance. It emphasises that success may be right around the corner, and with persistence any goal is attainable. When you decide to give up, you never truly realise how close you might be to achieving success;

    4. (iv)

      Leadership skills: Leading diverse individuals from various backgrounds towards a shared purpose or aim; and

    5. (v)

      Team work: Collaborating within a team with a shared objective, placing others before oneself, and setting aside personal differences.

  3. (c)

    Source of income: Sports have evolved into an international business, offering diverse economic prospects to numerous individuals in society, especially the younger generation. Engaging in sports enables people to find employment opportunities as coaches, educators, referees, administrators, legal experts, analysts, agents and athletes;

  4. (d)

    Rehabilitation: Engaging in sports can serve as a means to enhance the overall physical, mental, and psychological health of individuals. For example, sports programmes are designed to aid the recovery of people having physical and mental disorders; and

  5. (e)

    Cultural transmission: Sports can serve as a means of passing societal culture from one generation to the next. For example, during football matches, spectators display their cultural identity by wearing traditional attire, singing traditional songs, and conveying important messages that can be adopted by younger generations.
