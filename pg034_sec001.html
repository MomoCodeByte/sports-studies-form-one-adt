Push-ups

Push-ups strengthen the core muscles and the shoulders. Push-ups can be done anywhere; as no special equipment is required. The following is how you can perform it:

(i) Lay on your tummy to the floor;

(ii) Place the hands flat on the ground on either side of the trunk;

(iii) Plant the toes on the ground and use the arms to push the body up into a plank position;

(iv) Keep the back flat and the gluteus compressed together as you lower the back down, so the chest makes contact with the floor; and

(v) Return to the plank position by pushing the weight down through the palms.
