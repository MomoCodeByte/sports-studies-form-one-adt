Middle distance running

Middle distance running events are track races that cover longer distance than sprints and are measured in laps on the running track. This race requires an athlete to build both general and specialised endurance for the energy demands of respective races. This race combines slow and fast speeds in a given amount of time. In middle distance, athletes must maintain a consistent pace that enables reaching finishing point quickly. The race begins in a standing start and includes 800 m and 1500 m.

The 800 m racing

This is the running event on the track requiring an athlete to complete two (2) laps on a standard 400 m running track.

Starting 800 m race

The runners in 800 m start racing on the curved line. The maximum of 8 runners line up at the curved starting line to begin the race.

Upon the command “On your mark” runners in a standing position observe the following procedures:

(i)
A front foot is placed up on the starting line with the other foot about shoulder width behind;
(ii)
Weight is over the front foot; and
(iii)
Arms are in position to move in coordination with the legs, as shown in Figure 5.15.
